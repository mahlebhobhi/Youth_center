ABOUT MAHLAKA

Supporting young people to move forward.

Our website presents the centre's purpose, activities and ways for young people and community members to connect.

OUR STORY

A youth-focused space built around opportunity.

Mahlaka Youth Centre is presented in this student project as a community-focused youth organisation. The centre provides a welcoming space where young people can access development activities, practical learning and positive social opportunities.

The website keeps information clear and easy to find so that visitors can quickly understand the organisation and decide how they can participate.
